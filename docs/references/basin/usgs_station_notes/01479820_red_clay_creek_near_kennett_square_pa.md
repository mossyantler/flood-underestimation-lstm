Use in basin diagnosis:

Treat this as a Red Clay Creek mainstem station with clear official evidence for some upstream regulation, but not as a known major-reservoir outlet. Event-scale diagnosis should first use hydrograph timing, nearby Red Clay gauges, and rain-window structure. Small dams, water withdrawal, and point-source context can be mentioned as secondary caveats, especially for low-flow or dry-window behavior, but the station note alone does not justify a high-confidence reservoir-suppression diagnosis for large flood peaks.
