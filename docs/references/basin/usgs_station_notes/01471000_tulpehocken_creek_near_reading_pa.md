Station/source summary:

- Station name: `01471000 Tulpehocken Creek near Reading, PA`.
- Location: right bank in Berks County, Pennsylvania, 15 ft upstream from the covered bridge on Township Route 921, about 1.0 mi downstream from Cacoosing Creek, 2.5 mi upstream from the mouth, and 3.5 mi northwest of Reading.
- Drainage area: USGS lists `211 mi2`, close to the local CAMELSH/DRBC area of `557.10 km2`.
- Period of record: October 1950 to current year.
- Gage/datum: water-stage recorder and crest-stage gage. The current USGS station text lists datum `216.60 ft` above NGVD29, with satellite telemetry at the station.
- Cooperation/funding: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Philadelphia District, and Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection.
- Regulation/diversion remarks: current station text and the checked water-data report PDFs state that flow has been regulated since April 1979 by Blue Marsh Lake, station `01470870`, about `3.9 mi` upstream.
- Rating/quality caveat: the water-year 2001 PDF reports good records and no estimated daily discharges for that water year. The PDF also separates statistics for water years `1980-2001` as the regulated period and `1951-1979` as the pre-regulation period.
- Flood-history context: the water-year 2001 PDF lists the pre-regulation June 23, 1972 peak separately from the regulated-period summary statistics. This supports treating large modern storm hydrographs as post-Blue-Marsh regulated records rather than directly comparable to the pre-1979 flood regime.

Use in basin diagnosis:

Treat this station as a regulated Tulpehocken Creek outlet downstream from Blue Marsh Lake, but not as identical to the Blue Marsh Damsite gauge. The station is farther downstream and below Cacoosing Creek, so event hydrographs can mix regulated reservoir release/attenuation with faster incremental tributary runoff. Event-specific operation claims should remain conservative because this note does not include Blue Marsh Lake operation logs for the storm dates.
