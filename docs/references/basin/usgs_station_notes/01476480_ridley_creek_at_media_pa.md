Station/source summary:

- Station name: `01476480 Ridley Creek at Media, PA`.
- Location: Delaware County, Pennsylvania, on the right bank about `400 ft` downstream from the U.S. Highway 1 / Baltimore Pike bridge at Media.
- Drainage area: USGS lists `30.5 mi2`, consistent with the local CAMELSH/DRBC area of `78.37 km2`.
- Period of record: the 2013 Water-Data Report describes surface-water records from October 1986 to September 1995, fragmentary records from October 1995 to December 1996, and January 1997 to the report year. The current inventory lists daily discharge data beginning `1986-10-01`.
- Gage/datum: the 2013 Water-Data Report describes a water-stage recorder and crest-stage gage with satellite telemetry and an approximate gage elevation of `110 ft` above NGVD29 from topographic map. The current inventory lists datum of gage as `100.0 ft` above NAVD88.
- Cooperation/funding: the current monitoring page lists operation in cooperation with the Delaware River Basin Commission. The 2013 report also identifies Delaware River Basin Commission cooperation.
- Regulation/diversion remarks: the checked USGS Water-Data Report states that diversion records are provided by Aqua Pennsylvania Water Company and notes upstream diversion by Aqua Pennsylvania Water Company during the period of record. This is direct station-context evidence for municipal diversion, but not a direct record of event-scale reservoir release. The local StreamStats cache reports `isRegulated = false`.
- Rating/quality caveat: the 2013 Water-Data Report describes discharge records as poor. The 2003 PDF notes that the 2003 maximum peak flow was based on a rating curve extended above `1,600 ft3/s` using a slope-area measurement. This supports cautious language for extreme peak magnitudes.
- Water-Year Summary availability: the USGS Water-Year Summary Reports page indicates report availability for recent water years, and the 2013 PDF was used as the report-style source. The older 2003 PDF was also checked because it preserves the station text and peak-flow/rating context.

Use in basin diagnosis:

Treat this station as a small to midsize suburban Piedmont basin with documented upstream municipal diversion and high local water-use proxy, but without a StreamStats regulation flag or a major-dam proxy. Event-scale `obs < sim` should therefore be described as a possible combination of convective precipitation footprint mismatch, local storage/detention, antecedent state, and diversion context rather than as confirmed reservoir suppression. Dry-window `obs > sim` or release-like claims require separate operation records and are not supported by the checked station sources alone.
