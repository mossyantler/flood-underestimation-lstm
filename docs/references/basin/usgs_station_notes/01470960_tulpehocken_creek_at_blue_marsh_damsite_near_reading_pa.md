Station/source summary:

- Station name: `01470960 Tulpehocken Creek at Blue Marsh Damsite near Reading, PA`.
- Location: right bank in Berks County, Pennsylvania, about 1.0 mi upstream from Rebers Bridge and Plum Creek, 1.0 mi east of Blue Marsh, and 5.5 mi northeast of Reading.
- Drainage area: USGS lists `175 mi2`, close to the local CAMELSH/DRBC area of `461.38 km2`.
- Period of record: May 1965 to current year.
- Gage/datum: water-stage recorder, crest-stage gage, and water-quality monitor. Current station text lists datum `229.2 ft NAVD88` from GNSS survey; before February 19, 2025 the datum was reported as `230.06 ft NGVD29`. Prior to November 25, 1974, the water-stage recorder was at a site about 0.3 mi downstream at the same datum.
- Cooperation/funding: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Philadelphia District.
- Regulation/diversion remarks: USGS station text and the Water-Year Summary say flow has been regulated since April 1979 by Blue Marsh Lake, station `01470870`, about 0.8 mi upstream. The water-year 1999 PDF gives the same regulation remark.
- Rating/quality caveat: the water-year 1999 PDF says records were good except estimated daily discharges, which were poor. Recent Water-Year Summary remarks describe recent records as generally fair/good depending on year, with estimated discharges lower quality.

Use in basin diagnosis:

Treat this station as a directly regulated outlet below Blue Marsh Lake. Event-scale differences between precipitation-driven model output and observed streamflow can reflect flood attenuation, delayed release, or step/plateau-like managed outlet behavior. This note does not include reservoir operation logs for the specific storm dates, so event-specific operation claims should remain conservative.
