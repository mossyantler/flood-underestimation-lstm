Use in basin diagnosis:

Treat this station as a moderately developed White Clay Creek mainstem basin with documented municipal water-supply influence. The station note supports caution for low-flow, dry-window, or Q99-only hydrograph interpretation and makes managed withdrawals a credible secondary context. It does not by itself justify labeling every flood-event mismatch as reservoir suppression; event hydrographs, seed-level simulations, and nearby White Clay gauges should still decide whether a given stress event is a rain-linked flood, a false-positive model response, or a possible managed-flow signal.
