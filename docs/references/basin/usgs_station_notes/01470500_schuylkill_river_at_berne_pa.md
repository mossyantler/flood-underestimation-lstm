Station/source summary:

- Station name is `Schuylkill River at Berne, PA`; station number `01470500`.
- Location is Berks County, Pennsylvania, Hydrologic Unit `02040203`, on the right bank upstream from the Township Route 558 bridge at Berne, about `0.5 mi` upstream from Mill Creek and `6.5 mi` downstream from Little Schuylkill River.
- USGS drainage area is `355 mi2`, consistent with the CAMELSH/DRBC area of about `925 km2`.
- Period of record begins in August 1947 and extends to current published data on the USGS page.
- Current gage description lists a water-stage recorder, crest-stage gage, and heated tipping-bucket precipitation gage. Current datum is `309.94 ft NAVD88`; the station page notes that before November 8, 2022 the datum was reported as `310.65 ft NGVD29`.
- USGS remarks state that there is some regulation at low flow by mine pumpage and Still Creek Reservoir. The 2003 annual water-data PDF gives the same low-flow regulation remark and identifies Still Creek Reservoir as station `01469200`, about `25 mi` upstream.
- Records in the 2003 PDF are marked good except estimated daily discharges, which are poor. The PDF notes the 1972 maximum peak flow was from a rating curve extended above `20,800 ft3/s`.
- The current station page notes that 30-minute precipitation data are temporary and not archived for long-term retrieval; the CAMELSH event analysis uses the local gridded `Rainf` forcing, not this temporary station precipitation feed.

Use in basin diagnosis:

Treat this station as a large Schuylkill mainstem gauge with documented low-flow regulation context, not as a station with direct USGS evidence of flood-control regulation for the stress events. Local hydromod attributes show many dams and storage in the basin, but individual event interpretation should remain conservative unless hydrograph timing, model residuals, and nearby-gauge comparison support a stronger storage/routing claim.
