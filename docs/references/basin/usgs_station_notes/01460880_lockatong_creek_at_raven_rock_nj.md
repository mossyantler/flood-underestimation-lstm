- Expanded site metadata gives latitude `40.4161111`, longitude `-75.01777778`, HUC `02040105`, altitude about `100 ft NAVD88`, and drainage area `22.9 mi2`. This is consistent with the CAMELSH/DRBC area near `59.5 km2`.

- USGS SIR 2013-5234 identifies the 2011 Hurricane Irene peak at this station as a new peak of record and marks the station's flow-regulation field as `NR`, meaning no regulation in that table.

Use in basin diagnosis:

Treat this as a small, mostly unregulated Lockatong Creek gauge with limited observed discharge coverage. Local hydromod proxies and StreamStats also do not point to dam regulation. Event-level differences should therefore be interpreted first as rainfall-runoff timing, storm footprint, model peak/recession behavior, or coverage limitations, not as reservoir/dam suppression unless new station-specific evidence is found.
