Station/source summary:

- Station name is `Lehigh River at Walnutport, PA`; station number `01451000`.
- Location is Northampton County, Pennsylvania, Hydrologic Unit `02040106`, on the left bank near Walnutport and upstream from Trout Creek.
- USGS drainage area is `889 mi2`, consistent with the CAMELSH/DRBC area of about `2297 km2`.
- Period of record begins in October 1946 and extends to current published data on the USGS page.
- Current gage description lists a water-stage recorder, crest-stage gage, and heated tipping bucket precipitation gage. Current datum is `349.53 ft NAVD88`; the station page notes that before October 15, 2022 the datum was published as `350.27 ft NGVD29`.
- USGS remarks state that flow is regulated by Wild Creek Reservoir, Penn Forest Reservoir, Francis E. Walter Reservoir, and Beltzville Lake. The 2003 annual water-data PDF gives the same regulation list and start dates: Wild Creek Reservoir since January 1941, Penn Forest Reservoir since October 1958, Francis E. Walter Reservoir since February 1961, and Beltzville Lake since February 1971.
- Records in the 2003 PDF are marked good except estimated daily discharges, which are poor. The PDF also notes an instantaneous low flow caused by upstream shutoff. This supports regulation context but does not provide event-specific operation records for the extreme-rain events analyzed here.

Use in basin diagnosis:

Treat this station as a regulated, large Lehigh mainstem gauge. Reservoir/storage context is directly supported by USGS station remarks and local hydromod attributes, but individual stress-event interpretation should remain conservative unless supported by hydrograph timing and nearby-gauge contrast.
