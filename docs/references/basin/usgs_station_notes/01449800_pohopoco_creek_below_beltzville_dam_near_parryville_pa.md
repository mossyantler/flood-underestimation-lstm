# 01449800 Pohopoco Creek below Beltzville Dam near Parryville, PA

Sources checked:

- USGS monitoring location page: https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-01449800/
- USGS legacy current-conditions station text: https://waterdata.usgs.gov/nwis/uv?legacy=1&site_no=01449800
- USGS water-data report PDF, water year 2000: https://pubs.usgs.gov/wdr/2000/dela-00/wdr_del-2000/pdfs/01449800.pdf

Station/source summary:

USGS station text identifies this site as `01449800 POHOPOCO CREEK BELOW BELTZVILLE LAKE NEAR PARRYVILLE, PA`, drainage area `96.4 mi2`, on the right bank `0.4 mi downstream from Beltzville Dam` and `2.3 mi northeast of Parryville`. Period of record is October 1967 to current year.

The current USGS station page states that flow is regulated for the entire period of record by Wild Creek Reservoir, Penn Forest Reservoir, and Beltzville Lake. It also notes an upstream diversion from Tunkhannock Creek to Wild Creek Basin since October 1969. The water-data report PDF gives the same regulation/diversion context and additionally names Wild Creek Reservoir as station `01449700`, Penn Forest Reservoir as station `01449400`, and Beltzville Lake as station `01449790`; it describes Wild Creek and Penn Forest as reservoirs for the City of Bethlehem.

Gage/datum note: current USGS station text reports a water-stage recorder, crest-stage gage, water-quality monitor, and concrete control, with datum `491.4 ft NAVD88`; prior to November 2022 and April 18, 2023, the datum was reported as `492.05 ft NGVD29`. The water-year 2000 PDF used `492.05 ft above sea level`.

Data-quality note: the water-year 2000 report says discharge records were good except estimated daily discharges, which were poor. The current page labels current data provisional as usual for real-time USGS data.

Use in basin diagnosis:

This station should be treated as a regulated, managed-flow outlet rather than a natural rainfall-runoff gauge. Reservoir storage/release and the upstream Tunkhannock-to-Wild-Creek diversion are direct source-supported explanations for attenuated peaks, delayed plateaus, step-like flow changes, and dry-window observed flow not explained by local hourly `Rainf` alone. Event-level claims should still remain conservative because this note does not include operation logs for the specific storm dates.
