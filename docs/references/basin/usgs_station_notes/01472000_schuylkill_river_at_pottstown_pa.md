Station/source summary:

- Station name: `01472000 Schuylkill River at Pottstown, PA`.
- Location: right bank in Montgomery County, Pennsylvania, 75 ft upstream from the Hanover Street bridge in Pottstown and 0.3 mi downstream from Manatawny Creek.
- Drainage area: USGS lists `1,147 mi2`, close to the local CAMELSH/DRBC area of `2,971.815 km2`.
- Period of record: October 1927 to current year.
- Gage/datum: water-stage recorder and crest-stage gage. Recent station text lists datum `116.92 ft NAVD88`; before November 9, 2022 the datum was reported as `117.86 ft NGVD29`. Earlier site history includes a non-recording gage in 1927-1928, a recorder 100 ft downstream from November 1928 to December 1972, and a non-recording gage 1.0 mi downstream from December 1972 to May 1974 at a datum 2.83 ft lower.
- Cooperation/funding: station operation is supported by the Delaware River Basin Commission, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Philadelphia District, and Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection. The Water-Year Summary also lists USGS cooperation with these agencies.
- Regulation/diversion remarks: USGS station text and Water-Year Summary state that flow is regulated by Blue Marsh Lake (`01470870`) since April 1979, Still Creek Reservoir (`01469200`) since February 1933, and Lake Ontelaunee. The water-year 2003 PDF gives the same regulation remark.
- Rating/quality caveat: the water-year 2003 PDF says records were good except estimated daily discharges, which were fair. Recent Water-Year Summary remarks classify water years 2014-2016 as fair except estimated daily discharges, which are poor, and water years 2017-2024 as fair except estimated discharge, which is poor.
- Local setting caveat: because the gage is just downstream from Manatawny Creek, event-scale increments between upstream Schuylkill gauges and Pottstown can include local tributary inflow in addition to mainstem routing and reservoir regulation.

Use in basin diagnosis:

Treat this station as a regulated mainstem Schuylkill outlet with substantial upstream reservoir context and a nearby tributary confluence. Event-scale mismatches between precipitation-driven model output and observed streamflow can reflect reservoir timing/attenuation, downstream routing, and local Manatawny Creek contribution. This note does not include reservoir operation logs or a local Manatawny Creek hourly CAMELSH series for the diagnosed storm dates, so event-specific release claims should remain conservative.
