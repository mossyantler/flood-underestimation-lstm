The official station name is Marsh Creek near Downingtown, PA. The USGS site service gives a drainage area of `20.3 mi2`, which is consistent with the local CAMELSH/DRBC area of `52.0632 km2`.

The current USGS monitoring page metadata places the site in Chester County, Pennsylvania, on Marsh Creek, with altitude `284.8 ft` above NAVD88 from a GNSS survey. The page includes a datum revision note: prior to February 19, 2025, the gage datum had been reported as `280 ft` above NGVD29.

The current USGS station observation text states that flow has been completely regulated since November 1973 by Marsh Creek Reservoir. This is the key station-context fact for basin diagnosis.

The 2002 USGS Water-Data Report site sheet gives the older station description: left bank `1,000 ft` downstream from Marsh Creek Dam, `0.2 mi` upstream from the mouth, and `3.0 mi` north of Downingtown. It lists period of record from June 1973 to current year, a water-stage recorder, crest-stage gage, and concrete control, and repeats that flow is completely regulated since November 1973 by Marsh Creek Reservoir, station `01480684`. The 2002 sheet rates the water-year records as good and reports no estimated daily discharges for that water year. It also includes a monthly contents-change row for Marsh Creek Reservoir, but no hourly gate, storage, or release-operation record was reviewed here.

## Diagnosis Use

For event-scale hydrograph diagnosis, use this station as a high-confidence regulated outlet. The safe claim is regulation/storage control by Marsh Creek Reservoir and Marsh Creek Dam, not a specific gate operation for any individual storm unless separate operation records are obtained.
