Station/source summary:

- Station name: Lackawaxen River at Rowland, PA.
- Location: concrete highway bridge on SR 1003, river mile 4.02, about 0.5 mi east of Rowland, Pike County, Pennsylvania.
- Drainage area: 589 square miles, consistent with the local CAMELSH attribute area of about 1,528 km2.
- Period of record: water year 1949 as a low-flow partial-record station, miscellaneous measurements during water years 1989-2007, and continuous records from July 2007 onward.
- Gage: water-stage recorder, crest-stage gage, and water-quality monitor. Datum is 668.83 ft above NAVD88. The page notes a prior gage location about 0.3 mi upstream at a different datum before October 1988.
- Regulation/diversion remarks: USGS reports regulation by Prompton Reservoir, General Edgar Jadwin Reservoir at high flow, and Wallenpaupack Lake upstream. The legacy page also notes rapid stage changes below the hydroelectric plant and gives a full-capacity plant discharge scale of 1,708 ft3/s.
- Rating/quality caveat: Water-Year Summary remarks state records are generally good, while estimated discharge is poor.
- Cooperation/funding context: USGS Pennsylvania Water Science Center record, with Pennsylvania DEP and Brookfield Renewable/BIF III Holtwood listed on USGS pages.

Interpretive use for basin dissection:

This station should be treated as a regulated mainstem Lackawaxen site. Event-scale model-observation differences can reflect both flood attenuation/storage and operation-related release or stage changes, so causal claims should separate rainfall-runoff signal from managed-flow context.
